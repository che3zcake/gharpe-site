GharPe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

GharPe is in private testing. It is not open to the public, and no real money moves through it. We will update this page before GharPe handles real payments.

Who we are

GharPe is a service for sending money home, used through WhatsApp. During testing it is run by its founding team. Contact: che3zcake@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number and profile name, which WhatsApp shares with us when you message GharPe.
  • The messages you send to GharPe and the replies we send you.
  • Your account details, which you enter on GharPe's own secure pages: your legal name, email address and phone number, and a password, which GharPe stores only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Your recipients' details: their name, address, bank account number (or UPI ID) and IFSC code. GharPe keeps only a masked copy of the account number.

GharPe never asks for card numbers, passwords, PINs, one-time codes or ID numbers inside WhatsApp. For now, when you add a new recipient, GharPe asks for their bank account number and IFSC code in the chat, one question at a time; this will move to a secure WhatsApp form. GharPe saves the conversation with card, account and ID numbers masked.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and carry out what you ask for in the conversation.
  • To send you updates about a transfer you started. Reply stop and we stop sending you messages you did not ask for. When you stop, you can choose to keep getting approval requests and results for transfers you started. We always reply to your own messages. Send start to turn updates back on.
  • To test and improve GharPe.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Meta (WhatsApp), which carries messages between you and GharPe under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • Cybrid, the payment platform GharPe is being tested on, in its test ("sandbox") environment. When you set up a transfer, Cybrid receives your legal name, plus your recipient's name, address, bank account number (or UPI ID) and IFSC code. During testing no real money moves.
  • Persona, which runs the identity check for Cybrid, and Plaid, which links your US bank account for Cybrid. Each receives what you enter on its own screens, such as your ID document, date of birth, address or bank sign-in. GharPe does not see or store those details.
  • OpenRouter, which passes your message to an AI model, and TypeSafe, whose Jev model reads it to work out what you are asking about, such as fees, delivery time or where your transfer is. The model only picks the topic; GharPe writes every reply. It receives up to 300 characters of one message and nothing else: not your name, phone number or account details. It never receives a message from a chat that is not linked to an account, or a message that looks like it holds a card, bank account, ID or UPI number, or a PIN, one-time code, CVV or password. GharPe sends it only on terms under which your message is not kept or used to train AI models.
  • Railway, which hosts GharPe's servers and database.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep test data only as long as testing needs it, and delete it when testing ends or when you ask, whichever comes first.

Deleting your data

Email che3zcake@gmail.com with the subject GharPe data deletion and the WhatsApp number you used. We will delete your information and confirm within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
